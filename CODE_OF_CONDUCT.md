# Code of Conduct

This is a personal portfolio project, but anyone who visits, contributes, or opens an issue here is part of a small community. This document explains the values I try to uphold and what I expect from everyone who participates.

---

### My Pledge

We want participation in this project to be respectful, welcoming, and free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, experience level, background, gender identity or expression, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, or appearance.

### My Values

**Respect and Kindness**
* Treat others the way you'd want to be treated, especially beginners.
* Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and about the work, not the person.
* Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
* Disagree openly, but without hostility, mockery, or personal attacks.

**Good Coding Practices**
* Write code that is clear, readable, and maintainable. Future contributors (including future you) will thank you.
* Prefer simple solutions over clever ones.
* Test your changes and avoid breaking existing behavior.
* Comment on the "why," not just the "what."
* Respect accessibility and performance. A site should work for everyone, on any device.
* Be open to code review, and be generous when reviewing others.

**Honesty and Integrity**
* Give credit where it is due. Do not pass off someone else's work as your own.
* Respect software licenses, including those of dependencies, fonts, images, and other assets.
* Be upfront about mistakes, limitations, and uncertainty. "I don't know" is a fine answer.
* Never plagiarize code, designs, or content.

**Ethical Software Development**
* Build software that helps people and does not deceive or exploit them.
* Respect user privacy. Collect only the data you truly need, and never expose personal information.
* Do not introduce malware, tracking without consent, dark patterns, or deliberately harmful behavior.
* Report security issues responsibly (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) rather than exploiting them.
* Consider the impact of your work on others, including people who may be affected indirectly.

**Responsible Use of AI**
AI tools (such as code assistants and chatbots) can be useful, and using them here is welcome, provided you use them responsibly:

* **You are accountable for what you submit.** Review, understand, and test any AI*generated code or text before contributing it. "The AI wrote it" is not an excuse for bugs, security flaws, or errors.
* **Be transparent.** If a contribution was substantially generated with AI, say so in your pull request.
* **Check licensing and originality.** AI output can reproduce copyrighted or licensed material. Do not submit anything you don't have the right to share.
* **Protect private data.** Never paste secrets, credentials, or other people's personal information into AI tools.
* **Don't fabricate.** Do not use AI to invent fake references, reviews, credentials, or project claims.
* **Keep humans in the loop.** AI should support your thinking and learning, not replace it. Be ready to explain your changes in your own words.
* **No AI*driven spam.** Mass*produced, low*effort issues or pull requests generated by AI without human review will be closed.

 Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, discrimination, insults, or derogatory comments
* Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
* Threats, intimidation, or doxxing (publishing others' private information)
* Trolling, spam, or deliberately disruptive behavior
* Plagiarism or knowingly violating licenses
* Submitting malicious or deceptive code or content
* Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication related to this project. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

### Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by emailing **kelsengile.dev@gmail.com** with the subject line "Code of Conduct." Include links or screenshots if you can. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.
